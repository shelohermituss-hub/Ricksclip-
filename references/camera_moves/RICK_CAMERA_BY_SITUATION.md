# How Rick's camera tells the story, by situation (2026-11-16)

PRIVATE: the pictures in `pictures/` hold Rick's frames. Never publish them.

Sources: six of his films (roller coaster, cart army, railroads and pink cell, already tabled move by move in `episodes/armor-no-holes/refs/camera_study/CAMERA_STUDY.md`, plus painkillers and slot machines, tracked and read by eye today) against our two newest films (fake surgery v1 and lightning spear v2, the last approved). Same tracker for all (`tools/measure/cam_track.py`, 15 fps).

## 1. The short answer

Rick does not choose a camera move per noun. He chooses it per **story job**. Every situation has its own move, and the move is almost always **attached to something**: the thing that moves (cart, spark, car, pill), a line in the set (rail, corridor, list, wire), or the object being built. Then he **lands** and **rests** while the picture changes.

We already have most of the timing rules (fly, land, rest; few cuts; smooth). What our films are missing is this **story-job grammar**. Our builders frame each picture-plan row on its own, so explanation, set-up and acting all come out as the same thing: a new framing every 0.5-1 s.

| (tracker, whole film) | Rick, 6 films | Ours: spear v2 (approved) | Ours: surgery v1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| time flying | 31-80 % (most 47-58) | 24 % | 26 % |
| time at rest | 4-48 % (most 20-28) | 58 % | 61 % |
| median flight | 0.7-1.7 s | 0.4 s | 0.5 s |
| longest flight | 3.3-9.3 s | 1.5 s | 1.1 s |
| flights of 3 s or longer | 1-3 per film | 0 | 0 |
| time inside flights of 2 s or longer | 14-66 % | 0 % | 0 % |
| first flight | 0.2-2.1 s (5 of 6 under 1 s) | 0.4 s | 1.5 s |
| hard picture jumps per minute (tracker, approximate) | 1-5 (narrative films) | 10 | 44 |

So even our approved film has no long attached flight. That is the gap the table below fills.

## 2. Situation to move (with the picture for each)

| Story situation | What Rick's camera does | Example (seconds) | Ours |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HOOK** (0-4 s) | Rest 0.6-0.9 s close on the strange act, then a flight that REVEALS the big thing (pull back + crane up, or whip to it). First flight by 1 s. Two or three pictures by 4 s. | Coaster 0-2.7: hammering, then pull back x0.5 shows the bent loop over him. Slots 0-2: reels macro, then pull back to the man. | Spear: push to the spear tip then crane up the tower (good). Surgery: holds, hops, flash; no reveal. `pictures/01_hook.jpg` |
| **SET UP THE WORLD** (who, where, stakes) | ONE long flight along a line in the set that joins the places. No cuts. | Railroads 3.7-11.5: one 7.8 s flight, canyon rim, over the edge, across the desert, into the hall with the banner. | Surgery 12.5-17: four places in 4.5 s by cuts and hops. `02_setup.jpg` |
| **CHARACTER TALKS / MOCKER** | Rest 1.2-2.8 s on lip sync, slow creep in. On the angry or smug word: a short push in, or a slow orbit + crane down round the speaker. | Railroads 13.6-19.6: push in as the boss leans to the lens, orbit while his face goes purple. Coaster 4.7-7.0: push to the face, rest while the grin drops. | Spear 9-15: still two-shot, fine, but the camera adds nothing to the feeling. `03_mocker_talk.jpg` |
| **HERO DOES THE ACT / BUILDS** | Push in to the hands, then ONE flight over the object part by part (the attention chain: hands, part, part, result, faces). Macro inserts top-down. | Cart 17.5-22.7: one 5.3 s flight round the board, to the tubes, down the strings, back to his hands, across to the crowd. Pink 5.3-6.4: whip push x3.4 down into the paint bowl. | Surgery 3-5.5: wide, macro, flash as separate framings. `04_hands_build.jpg` |
| **EXPLANATION** ("You see...") | The camera TRAVELS the mechanism: fly into the object, then along it like reading (down a list, along a rail, with a highlight). Rests only where the picture itself changes (colour, counter, calendar). | Slots 17-25: into the machine on "crack it open", crane down the list, counter ticks on "over and over". Coaster 47.9-50.5: trucks with the highlight down the rail to the tight bottom. Railroads 38.6-41.9: a 3.3 s rest while the rail changes colour and the calendar flips. | Surgery 50-55: five framings in five seconds (charts, close, cabinet, printer). `05_explanation.jpg` |
| **ANALOGY** ("it's like...", "picture X as Y") | Dissolve into the analogy world while moving, then ONE long straight push (one-point perspective) to the target. The payoff runs the SAME path again with the change. Back to the real face on the punch. Or: the same move at two speeds to compare. | Painkillers 15-23.7: 8.7 s push through the front door and down the corridor to the HEAD room; 25-34: the same path again with the pills. Coaster 41.4-46.2: slow truck with the slow car, fast push with the fast car that flips. | No analogy in our last two films; armor's mug analogy hopped along a shelf. `06_analogy.jpg` |
| **TURN / REVEAL** ("but...", the trick) | Slow pull back + crane up to show the whole thing. Pull backs are 24-35 % of his flights and nearly all are reveals. | Coaster 22.2-25.1: pull back shows the loop being squeezed narrower. | Spear 15.5-18.6: crane up the white tower, Rick-like. `07_reveal_turn.jpg` |
| **CHANGE OF PLACE / TIME** | The flight continues THROUGH a 0.3 s dissolve; or a fly-through into a story object (door, machine, pupil, monitor); or a body or train wipes the lens. Light changes with the story clock. | Coaster 25.9-30.6: push, dissolve while flying, the same push goes on up the red carpet. Painkillers 15-17: into the house door. | Surgery 12: hard cuts. Spear: dissolves, mostly from a still camera. `08_transition.jpg` |
| **CLIMAX** (the idea works) | The camera RIDES WITH the moving thing in the longest flight of the film (3-9 s), then one fast push on the hit. Power effect on the word. | Cart 51.7-55.2: crane up with the spark, it splits into 82 flames. Coaster 52.3-59.9: up the loop with the cart. | Spear 50-54: the bolt runs down the wire, the camera shows it in separate framings instead of riding down with it. `09_climax.jpg` |
| **PUNCHLINE / LAST FRAME** | Pull back from the result so the hero stands calm and big in front of it, then creep in on the smug face into the loop. | Cart 64.4-66.8, coaster 61-63, railroads 64.3-65. | Spear: push to the spear, dissolve to frame 0 (good loop, no hero-plus-result picture). `10_ending.jpg` |

Interior acting stretches (pink cell) are the exception: the camera rests up to 70 % of the time, and each flight is fast and changes the size 2-3 times (room to hands, door to stopwatch).

## 3. CB's 12 camera-director points: what we have

| # | Point | We have it? | Where, and what is missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Continuous paths, several compositions per shot, no cut per sentence | **Have, not followed** | CB_RULES 11a-2b ("a picture-plan row is not a camera station"), 11f ONE FLYING TAKE, `film_plan.py check` cut gate. Our last four films still put one station per row. The tool still has a gate "word stations not at rest on their word" that pushes the camera to stop on every noun (the armor study asked to remove it). |
| 2 | Every move has a narrative purpose (reveal, track, inspect, compare, follow, reaction) | **Partly** | CAMERA_NOTE.md asks for "the reason for every move", but there is no list of purposes and nothing checks it. The situation table above is that list. |
| 3 | Keyframes on narration and action anchors: position, target, framing, focus, easing | **Partly** | Stations have pos, aim, framing, lens, word, easing. No focus distance per station. |
| 4 | Foreground geometry and real parallax; motivated dolly and tracking over random orbits | **Partly** | Rule "a big foreground object in every frame" (section 3). No check. `follow` can only follow a character, not a prop, an effect or a line in the set, so the long attached flights (spark, bolt, highlight) cannot be planned. |
| 5 | Smooth eased movement, short decaying shake | **Have** | 11f SMOOTH, CONTROLLED CAMERA (S-curve, about 40 px/f cap), shake 3-8 frames decaying, speed gates in `film_plan.py`. |
| 6 | Wide for space, medium for acting, close for clues, inserts for mechanisms | **Have** | Section 6 shot size, CLOSE-UPS ON WHAT THE VOICE NAMES, close-up study. |
| 7 | 2.5D character readability | **Have** | Camera-safe rig (default since today), `tools/camsafe/validate.mjs`, view rules, frame checks. |
| 8 | Track 3D positions, coherent blocking | **Have, one gap** | 11d2 ONE WORLD (apply_looks, same moment from two angles). Gap: tracking works for characters only (see point 4). |
| 9 | Validate each move at start, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4, end | **Partly** | `frame_checks` tests the RESTS. Nothing tests the middle of a flight (occlusion, head cut, caption band, prop visible). |
| 10 | Moving previews before approval, never stills alone | **Have** | 11a gate 2 (the hook moving), armor and Moonrakers lessons. |
| 11 | Intentional transitions; no excess cuts, decorative orbits, random shakes, unmotivated zooms | **Mostly** | Sections 6, 6b, 11f. Decorative orbits are not checked. |
| 12 | Camera never redesigns characters | **Have** | 11d2 "a pure camera move never turns, moves or re-poses a character", camsafe "never turn or squash a card". |
| + | Attention chain: face, hand, prop, consequence, reaction | **Missing as a written pattern** | Rick does exactly this (cart 17.5-22.7; coaster 12.9-16.1: crushed cart, owner's face, he grabs the hammer). |

## 4. Improvements (proposals; no rule file changed, CB decides)

1. **Story-job tag on every picture-plan row** (HOOK, SETUP, TALK, ACT, EXPLAIN, ANALOGY, TURN, PLACE CHANGE, CLIMAX, END). The camera-director picks the move from the table in section 2 for each tag and writes it in CAMERA_NOTE.md. Rows with the same tag in one place share ONE move. Fixes points 1 and 2.
2. **`follow` for things, not only people** (`film_plan.py`): follow a prop, an effect path or a set line (rail, wire, rope, corridor), with an offset and the camera-safe height limit. This is what makes the spark, bolt and highlight flights possible. Biggest single tool gain.
3. **Plan gates that match the story job** (`film_plan.py check`): at least 2 flights of 3 s or longer that name what they follow; every EXPLAIN and ANALOGY block inside one travel; the climax holds the film's longest flight; the end is a pull back. Remove "word stations not at rest on their word" (it forces a stop per noun; Rick's camera is at rest on only 20-30 % of the long words; many land while he flies toward the thing).
4. **Recipes as code pieces** (three.js only, no Blender): `hook_reveal`, `travel_line` (set-up and explanation), `analogy_corridor` (dissolve in, long push to the target, repeat with the change), `attention_chain` (face, hand, prop, consequence, reaction as one flight with 3-5 landings), `ride_with` (climax), `end_pullback`. Builders fill in the anchors from the built set.
5. **Check the middle of every flight**: run `frame_checks` and `camsafe/validate.mjs` at 0, 25, 50, 75 and 100 % of each travel (occlusion, head cut, caption band, hero prop visible, nobody passing behind a figure). Fixes point 9.
6. **Camera-safe limits built into the recipes**: long follows stay at character height (within 10 degrees down), never pass behind a figure (FACE_BEHIND), views come from apply_looks, never set by hand.
7. **Small additions**: a focus target per station for the world-layer depth of field (point 3); a foreground check on wides (point 4); an orbit needs a reason in CAMERA_NOTE.md (point 11).
8. **Proof before rules**: rebuild one stretch of fake surgery v2 (the "You see" explanation, 50-56 s) with items 1-4, and show it moving next to the slots explanation and our v1. Only then send the rule text to the crew thread.
