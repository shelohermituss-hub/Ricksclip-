## Story world
Today, a family back garden on a winter night (any small town). ONE connected world: the boy's garden seen from every side. Landmarks visible from every angle: the house back wall with ONE warm kitchen window and a back door with a porch lamp (west side of the garden), a picket fence with snow caps around the lawn, a bare tree with a branch framing the sky (north east), strings of colourful paper lanterns zig-zagging from the house eaves to the tree and to fence posts, a garden shed (south), far neighbour roofs with snow and a few warm windows, far pale hills in haze, moon disc.
Hero object: the fireworks rack in the middle of the lawn on a cleared patch of snow: a wooden launch rack (planks, bolts, legs pushed into the snow) holding TWELVE rockets in a row (paper wraps in burst colours, cone caps, sticks), one shared fuse line running along the front to a single lead fuse. The lighter: a chunky metal flip lighter, big enough to read in the fist.
Words in the world (short, hand-painted, from this story): rocket wraps carry "BOOM", "SKY KING", "12 SHOT"; a box by the rack "BIG BANG CO."; a small sign on the shed "DAD'S SHED". Nothing else.

## Shadows
Cast shadow: hard, long, from the key, full figure (shadow pass), on the snow. Contact shadow: a small, soft, DARK patch under each boot sole (about the foot's width, fading within a few cm) and under every rack leg, lantern post, shed corner, fence post; slight snow dent / footprints where he stands and kneels. Corners and wall-snow lines darken softly.

## Air and motion of the world

Lanterns sway a little on their strings, tassels swing. Thin cool haze toward the far roofs and hills. Breath puffs from the boy at the action words (cold night). After the bursts: smoke drifting, glitter falling.
