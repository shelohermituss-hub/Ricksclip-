# Cart army match: what it takes (2026-11-11)
## Places (ONE connected world)
1. Fort: grey block wall with battlements, arched gate with wooden doors, red-pillar gate pavilion with dark tiled roof, round tower with red cone roof, wall walk paved in big stone slabs with a wooden rail, yard with deep grass and a dirt path, blue tiger banners, lanterns.
2. Valley: terraced green and yellow fields, winding dirt road, river, tree clumps, layered mountains, cloud sky.
## Hero object (built in 8 states)
Board plain, board with holes (82), board on the two-wheel cart, arrows going in, paper tubes on shafts, strings at the back, strings twisted to one rope, coat over it, lit and glowing, empty after launch. Plus one arrow close-up model (tube, straps, arrowhead).
## Cast (our flat 2D puppets, new outfits)
Inventor (red quilted coat, later white undershirt), commander (blue dot armour, plume helmet, beard), 6-8 fort soldiers (kettle hats, coifs), enemy general (black armour, horned helmet, litter and parasol), enemy soldier in 4-5 variants for a crowd of about 300 (instanced cards, yellow shields, boar banners).
## Motion (library first, new moves to add)
Have: walk, run, laugh, shrug, hammer, cheer, point. Must make: hand drill crank, twist rope, slap, reach down, throw coat, climb on and lie flat, sleep, rip cloth off, light with torch, arms-crossed smug, march in a block, carry litter.
## Effects
Wood chips, dust on the march, light shaft from the arch, slap impact lines + flash + helmet flying, ground cracking into blocks, rain sheets + wet stone + lantern glow, ladders slamming, spark running on a rope, 82 flame tips with glow, fly-through, 82 smoke trails, row of blasts, big fireball.
## Lights
Day, pink dusk, blue rain night with warm lanterns, sunrise with long shadows, bright day.
## Camera and edit
36 pictures, 19 quick blends (kit has ep_dissolves from the roller coaster round 7; not yet on the PC kit), whip rise over the wall, long pull back over the fort, follow shots, fly-through.
## Biggest risks
Crowd of 300 at speed in three.js and Blender; the ground cracking; the smoke trails; a detailed fort that holds up at macro distance (grass, stone, wood).
