## Beats
- **Hook (1-3):** frame 0 is the strange act (three holes, water, plasters, nothing inside); the nurse mocks it.
- **Problem and escalation (4-9):** a knee op done all day → a colleague's question → 180 knees → 60/60/60 → a form and a sealed envelope → the fake op performed with a tape, a wiggle and a splash.
- **Turn (10-11):** two years later the fake patient climbs stairs; the real groups are no better; three level bars.
- **You see (12-14):** at word 161 of 228 = 70.6 % (Rick median 69.35 %). Comparison inside the story's world: a kid in the same hospital's waiting room, whose knee stops hurting with one plaster (callback to frame 0's plasters).
- **Payoff (15-16):** the money stops; the mocker's own line ("you forgot the operation") comes true and is the happy ending. Evening light returns to frame 0's for the loop.
