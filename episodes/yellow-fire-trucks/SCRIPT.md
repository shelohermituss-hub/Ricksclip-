# WHY FIRE TRUCKS TURNED YELLOW: SCRIPT v1 (2026-11-20)

Pattern: strange act at frame 0, mocker with one funny line, "you see" setup early and the reveal in the middle, counted proof, last line = a picture that proves the hero right and the mocker falls by his own action. Facts: FACTS.md.
The fireman, the chief and the last gag are our own cartoon figures around the checked mechanism; no real names.
