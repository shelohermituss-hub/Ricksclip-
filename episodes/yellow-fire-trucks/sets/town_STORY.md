# town_STORY.md: PLACE A, PINE HOLLOW (set-builder, 2026-11-20, written BEFORE the build; CB_RULES section 3 STORY-DRIVEN WORLD BUILDING)
Early 1970s small American town. One connected world: the fire station on the NORTH side of Main Street, the eye doctor's shop
right across from it on the SOUTH side, the crossing of Main and Elm on the station's east corner, the park on the crossing's
south-east corner. The water tower PINE HOLLOW stands north-east behind the station roofs, so it is seen from the shop, the yard,
the crossing and the park. Forbidden on the whole set: red brick, stop sign, yellow road lines, red flag, anything teal.

## Environmental evidence (what happened before)
- Lemon overspray mist on the yard round bay 1's apron (a fan of fine dots, heaviest under where the truck stands), a lemon boot
  print going toward the door: he has been at it a while. Masking paper offcuts and a tape roll by the reel.
- Oil stains under bay 2 (the old pumper leaks) and none under bay 1 (the new truck): old against new told by the floor.
- Tyre tracks out of bay 2 to the street, worn pale; bay 1's apron clean.
- The yard line repainted many times (a ghost of an older, wobblier line beside it): the Chief's own drill line for parking straight.
- The shop: a worn path on the floor from the door to the chair; the chart lit by its own small lamp; dust on the blind.
- The crossing: a scuffed tyre arc and a broken-off hubcap mark on the kerb (earlier near misses), one bent blade post.
- The park: a ball-shaped dent in the long grass, a worn patch under the swing, a kite stuck in the tree.

## Character-specific details
- Hero (eye doctor + fireman): his shop's window faces bay 1, the place he looks at from his chair; spectacles shingle hand-painted.
- Chief: a brass plate CHIEF on the door, his chair-cushion on the bench, his old pumper with oil under it; the yard line is his.
- Old-Timer: lunch tin hook by the bench, a sandwich wrapper in the bin. Rookie: a too-big spare helmet on the peg by the door.
- Milkman: the dairy sign is not in this place (his van is the prop).

## Optional environment gags (only where the story uses them)
- The water tower's lettering is PINE HOLLOW; the town's pride is visible from every view (no gag on it).
- One pigeon on the bay header (AM), moths at the street lamp (DU): the air life of the look bible.
- The shingle EYE DOCTOR swings (rows 9, 11); the station sign and the blades do NOT move (signs hang still).

## Continuity constraints
- Landmarks fixed: station (bays south), shop window facing it, crossing east, park SE, water tower NE, hills ring N and E.
- Bay 1 = the hero truck (lemon state per shot), bay 2 = the Chief's red pumper (it stays red; at dusk it reads grey-plum).
- The yard line is always there, straight, white, from the door step (x -9.6, y 20.6) to bay 2's nose (x 4.2, y 12.2) ... it ends where
  the Chief's truck nose stands, so row 57's line runs INTO the truck.
- Lights: AM sun low from the east-south-east (screen left in the yard wide looking north), DU sun set behind the west houses; at
  DU the street lamp, the station wall lamp, the shop lamp and about half the house windows are lit.
- Nothing teal, no red except the trucks (and the story's ball / toy); house paints cream, sage, butter-pale, white, pale blue-grey.
