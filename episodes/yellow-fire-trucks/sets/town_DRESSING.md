- Hose tower: 7 white louvres; 3 slit windows with sills; pulley beam with its wheel; 13-rung steel ladder; gable cap.
- Yard: the straight white yard line with a wobbly ghost line beside it; oil stains only under bay 2; lemon overspray fan in front of bay 1; wet puddle at the drain; weeds in the joints.
- Eye shop: venetian blind half down; spectacles shingle on 2 chains; navy-and-cream striped awning; OPEN card on the door glass; eye chart R / ED / GOES / FIRST.
- Crossing: chipped white stop bar; navy blades on both faces; sodium globe under a dark head; mailbox; 4-insulator crossarms with sagging wires.
- Park: one fence gap; rope swing with a worn patch under it; real grass blades; dandelions; a PINE HOLLOW PARK sign.
- Water tower: X braces at 3 levels; balcony with 32 posts and rail; lettered band PINE HOLLOW; cone roof with finial; ladder.
