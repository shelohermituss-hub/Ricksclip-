# PLACE C, THE MAP: story (set-builder, written before the build, 2026-11-21). S4, line 7, 25.65-32.30 s
"So in the nineteen-seventies, cities started painting their trucks lime yellow. Plenty of firemen hated them."

## Required location
A 1970s folded road map of the lower 48 states, spread on a worn wooden table and treated as a tabletop model: 32 x 20 m in set metres
(a real 0.91 x 0.57 m map at x35). Bleached map day (LOOK_BIBLE "map/NO": proof, neutral): high white sun, pale sky bounce, hard small shadows.

## Essential action props (rows 22-25, 28)
- The printed map: interstates in navy with cream shields, US routes brown, state lines dashed plum, rivers, coast lining, hand-lettered city labels, title block U.S.A. ROAD MAP / 1970s (row 22 word in the world), legend with a FIRE STATION key.
- Pop-up fire-station models (card, butter-cream brick print, navy trim, hose tower, sign "<CITY> F.D.") at 49 city dots; two roll-up bay doors each (row 23: the door wave west to east), a truck nose in every bay (truck_light, red, then lime #E3EA22: rows 24-25).
- Pine Hollow home station model (sign PINE HOLLOW FIRE DEPT., the same layout as the town: two bays, hose tower) standing on a printed street plan (MAIN ST, ELM ST, PARK, WATER TOWER) so the sink of row 25 lands on "the paper lines becoming its yard".
- The DALLAS label (biggest city label), circled in pencil, with I-30 arriving from Texarkana: the S5 glide (row 28); a sand blend disc (control SAND 0..1) for "the paper ground blends to sand".

## Interactive surfaces
Bay doors (open 0..1 per station, wave by longitude), truck paint per station (red/lime), the sand disc, the paper surface (cards and painters stand on it at station scale).

## Environmental evidence (what happened before)
A coffee ring over Wyoming-Nebraska; the pencil circle round DALLAS; a pencil dashed route Pine Hollow -> Little Rock -> Texarkana -> Dallas; a pencil note OUR TOWN with an arrow at
Pine Hollow; pencil tick marks beside every station city (the idea spreading, counted by hand); worn crease crossings, two strips of old tape; fold creases in 3D (8 x 4 panels), curled corners.

## Character-specific details
Whoever owns the map is a fire-house planner: ticks per city, the home town circled as OUR TOWN, Dallas circled (the test that is coming in S5).
On the table: a brass compass (sea-dog compass, Poly Haven) on the Atlantic, a magnifying glass on the margin, a measuring tape: tools of someone checking a spread.

## Optional environment gags
One station door stuck half up (Denver) while the others roll (PROPS.md: "one door stuck half up"). The compass needle points at Dallas.

## Continuity constraints
- The home station model matches the town (two bays, hose tower on the west side, PINE HOLLOW FIRE DEPT.); no red brick, no yellow road lines, no red flag, nothing teal.
- The only lime on the set is the trucks' paint; the only red is the trucks before the wave. Signs and labels hang still, spelled right.
- Anchors (sets/map.js ANCHORS) come from one geography file (scripts/map_geo.py): Blender and the kit share every number.
