# lamproom_STORY.md: PLACE D, THE LAMP ROOM BEHIND THE EYE (set-builder, 2026-11-21, written before the build)

## Required location
Inside "your" eye (the viewer's), behind the pupil: a cartoon machine room, not flesh. A plum-indigo dome (LOOK_BIBLE LR, `#5B3F92` darker) whose
wall is a 1970s scoreboard of small round lamps; in front, the back of the iris as a pleated ring (curtain-shutter pleats) around a round pupil window.
Through the window: the REAL dusk crossing of Pine Hollow (town.blend rendered from the viewer's kerb, not a painting): the red pumper on the crossing,
nose to us, beacon lit; the hero small at the kerb in teal; the station, the street lamp, the MAIN ST blade. Rows 36-39, 40.24-43.94 s.

## Essential action props
- The lamps: three kinds (red `#D3141C`, lemon-green `#E3EA22`, blue), three states (on, flickering, off/dead). Red lamps are split into 4 wave
  groups by angle round the eye axis (row 38: off in 4 posed steps on "RED", 42.68). One red lamp flickers on "lose" (42.48).
- The iris: 48 pleated blades on a ring, opening 0..1 widens the pupil (row 37 "dim" 41.42; row 39 exit 43.02).
- The pupil window: two pictures (truck red / truck grey), cross-faded with the wave; a dim knob for "the light outside falls".
- The brass relay box (row 37 near thing): three switch rows, the red row drops one switch group per wave step. Big and close, lower left.
- The entry ring: a chrome porthole at the back of the dome, the headlamp's own profile (rim, bezel ribs, 3 screws), on the eye axis.

## Interactive surfaces
Lamps (wave, flicker), iris blades (open), window (red to grey, dim), relay switches (drop), entry ring (the camera passes through it), pupil (exit).

## Environmental evidence
Dead lamps in the rows (about 6 %, dark glass), a few lamps flickering already (old bulbs), cable bundles tied with clamps running down the
meridians to the relay box, patched housing plates, a ladder rail along the floor ring for the lamp man (nobody shown), rivet seams on the dome plates.

## Character-specific details
The eye is the viewer's, so no owner's things; the hero's presence is through the window (teal, holding his toy). The chrome of the entry ring
and the pupil lip repeat the pumper's headlamp chrome: the hero object is the door both ways.

## Optional gags (only if they serve)
