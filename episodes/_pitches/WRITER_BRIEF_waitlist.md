## Write
1. `episodes/<film>/SCRIPT.md`, in the elevator table format: line, speaker, light/place, what we see (silent test); then beats, per-line effects and gags, cast (at most 3 memorable people, era-true), places and light, and the ending twist (the pick and one alternative, one line each on why).
   - Rick's pattern:
     - frame 0 is the strange act, with ONE hero big in frame;
     - a mocker with one funny quoted line;
     - escalation with numbers you can see;
     - "You see" at about 70-80 %, explained with an everyday comparison that stays in the story's world (CB moved the elevator's comparison into the hotel);
     - a last line that flips frame 0 or the mocker, a limit or a reversal, acted so it reads with the sound off.
   - 200-230 words, about 60-65 s. Told like a 10-year-old to a friend.
2. `episodes/<film>/voice/voice_text_v1.txt`.
   - Every clause gets 2-4 energy and emotion tags.
   - Quoted lines are the SAME narrator in another TONE: tags for attitude, emotion and pace only. Never an accent, an age or a "voice" type (CB: "dont use someone else voice").
3. `episodes/<film>/STATE.md`: at most 20 lines of handover.

Real people: never their likeness. Use stand-in cartoon characters, and no brand logos. A real name may be said once only if FACTS.md marks it TRUE and it helps the story.

Do NOT record any voice; that waits for CB's OK. Do not render.

Report in at most 12 lines: the word count, the "you see" position as a %, the hook line, the mocker line, the twist line, and any line you are unsure of.
