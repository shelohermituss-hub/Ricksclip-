# SCRIPT v1: The Man Who Ate An Airplane (30 s, about 85 words, voice "rick", English)
1. In 1978, a French guy looked at an airplane... and thought: lunch.
2. Michel Lotito cut a whole Cessna into tiny metal bites.
3. Every day, a glass of oil, a big jug of water... and about two pounds of airplane.
4. Wings. Wheels. Seats. Even the tires.
5. Doctors X-rayed him and found a stomach lining so thick, the metal just slid through.
6. It took him two years to finish the plane.
7. And for dessert? Bikes, shopping carts, TVs... and a coffin.
8. Guinness called it the strangest diet ever. Honestly? Still better than airline food.
Words: 88 (spoken lines only).
