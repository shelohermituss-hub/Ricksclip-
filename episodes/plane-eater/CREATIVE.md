## 0. The absurd truth and the cast this file assumes
- THE TRUTH: a polite Frenchman treats an airplane as FINE CUISINE: oil served like wine, a set table, dessert. He is never wild or greedy. The joke is how calm and correct he is about it.
- MICHEL (hero): a calm gourmet. Slow, deliberate, little finger up, never rushed, delighted by every bite. Fork always in the RIGHT hand, knife in the left (grip logic). He wears the film's one red: a red napkin tucked into his collar, the same red as the Cessna's stripe ("he wears his food").
- THE OWNER (background, the sceptic): the fussy owner of the Cessna, always polishing it with a yellow rag; arms crossed, slow reactions, mouth falls open. He is never the focus. He carries the running gag (idea 4.1).
- THE DOCTOR (line 5): glasses, a white coat, a serious man who trusts his X-rays until this one.
- (8a only, optional) a record official in a blazer. A cheaper version with no new person is given in the row.
