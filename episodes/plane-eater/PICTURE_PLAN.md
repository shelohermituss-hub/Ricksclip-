## Fixed for the whole film (proposals to the lead, binding once OK'd)
- Signature colour TOMATO RED `#D8342A` on Michel only (bow tie, napkin checks). Cessna 150: white, navy stripes, tail letters F-MIAM (French register; "miam" = "yum"). No red on any other person or big prop.
- Mouths: nobody speaks. "Lunch!" is Michel's THOUGHT: lips smack, no open mouth. All lines are NARRATOR.
- Kid-safe: metal is always cut into neat small bites first (the fact), no sharp edge goes in whole, no blood, no pain; the coffin is a shiny empty showroom coffin. Fact vs fable stays in YOUTUBE.md (Snopes note).
- Hero object = the Cessna, built DOWN on screen step by step: whole (rows 1-8), bites cut (9), plate and scale (13), wing / wheels / seats / tires gone one per word (14-17), only the propeller left (23), gone (25), propeller back as a trophy (31-36).

- POWER 4 "FOOD." 33.38: the CHOMP into the airline tray: a bite-shaped hole appears, impact lines from the bite, shake 4 frames. Why: the punchline is the last act of the hero.
- EXTREME 1 "Lunch!": a thought bubble over him shows the WHOLE Cessna on a white dinner plate with a sprig of parsley and a lemon wedge, propeller still turning.
- EXTREME 2 "years": the wall calendar sheds two years of pages in a storm that fills the hangar (pages 1978 -> 1980 swirl round him like snow while he keeps eating, calm).
- EXTREME 3 "Bikes ... coffin": dessert is a wedding-cake tower to the hangar roof: bikes, then nested carts, then TVs, then the coffin as the cake topper with one lit candle.
